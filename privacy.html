Privacy Policy

DharmaVera — by Thakhin DBA

Last updated: September 27, 2026, with a hackathon edition note added October 27, 2026.

Thakhin DBA ("we," "our," or "us") operates the DharmaVera application and website at dharmavera.com/biorhythm.php. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information that you voluntarily provide when creating your profile:

  • Name — first name or nickname you choose to use
  • Date of birth — used to calculate biorhythm cycles and astrological data
  • Birth time (optional) — improves astrological precision
  • Birth city (optional) — used to determine geographic coordinates for chart calculations
  • Blood type (optional) — used only for the blood-type harmony feature, a Japanese popular tradition shown for entertainment. Blood type can be considered health information; we never share it, never use it for advertising, and you can remove it at any time in the Profile tab

We also collect data you enter while using the App:

  • Daily log entries — self-reported physical, emotional, intellectual, and luck scores (1–10) and optional personal notes
  • Life milestone dates — personal dates you optionally enter
  • Compatibility readings — names and dates of birth of people you choose to compare
  • Oracle Pulse readings — the question you type, the five component scores, the average answer score, and the date and time of the reading; these are saved only after you log in

2. How We Collect Information

All information is entered directly by you. We do not collect information from third-party sources, social networks, or advertising platforms. City geocoding (converting a city name to coordinates) is performed via OpenStreetMap Nominatim, a public service. Only the city name text you type is sent to that service — no other personal data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide the App's features:

  • Calculate and display your personal biorhythm cycles
  • Generate astrological charts (Chinese Zodiac, Mahabote, Western Synastry)
  • Display your daily log history and progress
  • Calculate compatibility readings when you request them
  • Track your membership level (Silver / Gold / Platinum) based on engagement points
  • Build your private Oracle Pulse history and calculate anonymous daily score averages for the administrator dashboard

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a secured MySQL database on a server located in the United States. We implement the following security measures:

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS (TLS encryption)
  • Each profile is protected by a 4-digit PIN together with the name and date of birth. The PIN is stored only as a secure one-way hash — nobody, including us, can read it. Repeated wrong PINs lock the profile for 15 minutes. Blood type is optional and is never used to verify identity
  • Database access is restricted to application credentials with limited permissions
  • Session cookies are marked HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Strict
  • All API endpoints are protected by CSRF tokens

No security method is 100% guaranteed. We encourage you not to enter highly sensitive information in notes or oracle-question fields.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time: sign in, open the Profile tab, and use Delete My Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You may also request deletion by email at app@southrule.com; we will process emailed requests within 30 days. See Delete your DharmaVera account for full details.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — city geocoding (city name text only; their privacy policy)
  • jsDelivr CDN — serves Bootstrap and Chart.js libraries (no personal data sent)

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising or tracking services.

Hackathon edition note - October 2026: In the hackathon demo on southrule.com, guest readings are saved only in your browser for the wave chart, not in a server account. The AI guidance sentence is generated by Muse Spark through the Meta Model API. Only numeric reading scores are sent to Meta. No name, date of birth, birth city, birth time, blood type, or typed question text is sent to Meta. The AI sentence is reflective guidance only, not medical, financial, or legal advice, not a prediction, and it does not change any score.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — update your birth time, city, or blood type at any time via the Profile tab
  • Deletion — request permanent deletion of your account and all data
  • Portability — request your daily log history in a readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at app@southrule.com.

9. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact app@southrule.com.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

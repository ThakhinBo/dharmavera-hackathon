Delete Your DharmaVera Account

DharmaVera — by Thakhin DBA

Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open My DharmaVera in the DharmaVera app or at dharmavera.com.
  2. Sign in with your name, date of birth and PIN.
  3. On the Profile tab, scroll to Delete My Account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Open My DharmaVera →

Or ask us by email

Email app@southrule.com with the subject “Delete my DharmaVera account”. Include the name and date of birth you used so we can find your profile. We process emailed requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, date of birth, birth time, birth city and coordinates, blood type, and personality type
  • Your daily logs and notes
  • Your life milestone dates
  • Your saved Harmony (compatibility) readings
  • Your Oracle Pulse questions and scores
  • Your rank, dUSD balance, streaks, likes, and follows

What is kept

No account data is kept. Standard web-server security logs, which record IP addresses and page requests but not your profile, may be kept for security purposes.

Anonymous totals are recalculated from the remaining members, so your readings no longer count toward them.

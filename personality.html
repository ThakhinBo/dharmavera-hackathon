About the 16 Personality Types

The 16 personality types grow out of Carl Jung's theory of psychological types and were popularised by the Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI®). DharmaVera uses the familiar four-letter codes as a simple reflection framework; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Myers-Briggs Company. Each person is described by four preferences across four dimensions:

E / I — Energy Direction
Extraversion vs Introversion
Where you get your energy. E = energised by people and activity. I = recharged by solitude and inner reflection.
S / N — Information Gathering
Sensing vs Intuition
How you take in the world. S = focuses on facts and present reality. N = drawn to patterns, possibilities, and the future.
T / F — Decision Making
Thinking vs Feeling
How you make decisions. T = logic, objectivity, and cause-effect analysis. F = values, harmony, and impact on people.
J / P — Lifestyle Approach
Judging vs Perceiving
How you organise your life. J = prefers structure, plans, and closure. P = prefers flexibility, spontaneity, and open options.
These are preferences, not abilities or fixed limits. All types are equally valuable, and no type pairing is inherently more successful in relationships.
